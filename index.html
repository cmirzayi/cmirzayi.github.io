Chloe Mirzayi, PhD

Investigator, CUNY Institute for Implementation Science in Population Health

I am an epidemiologist and data scientist. My work spans microbiome epidemiology, standards for reporting microbiome research, and large national cohort studies of HIV prevention and care among sexual and gender minority people.

I led the development of the STORMS checklist, a consensus reporting guideline for human microbiome studies, and helped build BugSigDB, a community-curated database of published microbial signatures. I also design and maintain data collection, management and curation systems for national cohorts and trials.

I received my PhD in Epidemiology from the CUNY Graduate School of Public Health & Health Policy, advised by Levi Waldron.
