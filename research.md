## Microbiome epidemiology & reporting standards

<div class="cards" markdown="0">
  <div class="card">
    <h3>STORMS checklist</h3>
    <p>Lead author of the Strengthening The Organization and Reporting of Microbiome Studies guideline, a 17-item checklist developed by a consortium of 101 authors from 89 institutions in 17 countries. Published in <a href="https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-021-01552-x"><em>Nature Medicine</em> (2021)</a>.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h3>BugSigDB</h3>
    <p>Second author of <a href="https://bugsigdb.org/">BugSigDB</a>, a community-editable database of manually curated microbial signatures from published differential abundance studies, built with dozens of student curators whom I helped mentor. Published in <a href="https://doi.org/10.1038/s41587-023-01872-y"><em>Nature Biotechnology</em></a>.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h3>STREAMS guidelines</h3>
    <p>Second author on the extension of STORMS to environmental and non-human host-associated microbiome studies. Published in <a href="https://doi.org/10.1038/s41564-025-02186-2"><em>Nature Microbiology</em> (2025)</a>.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h3>Antibiotics & the gut microbiome</h3>
    <p>My dissertation used causal and predictive modeling to study how antibiotic exposure affects the gut microbiome, including whether pre-exposure microbiome composition modifies antibiotic effects in infants.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## HIV prevention & population health cohorts

<div class="cards" markdown="0">
  <div class="card">
    <h3>Together 5000</h3>
    <p>A U.S. national, technology-mediated cohort studying HIV incidence and PrEP uptake among sexual and gender minority people. I have co-authored work on its recruitment, retention, PrEP use, and HIV seroconversion.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h3>AMETHST</h3>
    <p>The American Transformative HIV Study (2022–present), a national cohort of sexual and gender minority people, including those living with HIV.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h3>CHASING COVID</h3>
    <p>A national, community-based prospective cohort of U.S. adults tracking SARS-CoV-2 incidence, testing preferences, and the pandemic's social and mental health effects.</p>
  </div>
</div>
